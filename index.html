Venkatesh Mishra

I'm a second-year Computer Science PhD student at Arizona State University, where I'm advised by Dr. Chitta Baral. My current research interests lie in investigating the reasoning capabilities of Language Agents.

I completed my Master's in Computer Science from Arizona State University in 2024. Previously, I worked as a Software Consultant at Oracle.
